ELM369 Game Studio

Make, generate, preview, and play offline arcade games. No accounts, downloads, or external services required.

Maker / generator

Exports use the last generated project. Imports are validated data, never scripts. Seeded generation is repeatable with the same inputs.

Keyboard: arrows / WASD, Enter to start, Space to pause. Standard gamepad: D-pad / left stick, A to start, Start to pause. Touch: hold direction buttons. Arcade credits are free local counters, not payments.

Browser prototype for modern personal devices and compatible controller-equipped browsers. Static hosting supports online distribution, not multiplayer. Native consoles require vendor SDKs, certification, and separate adapters. Other genres, 3D, asset pipelines, and network play are not implemented.
